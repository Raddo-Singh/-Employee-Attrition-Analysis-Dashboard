# 📊 Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard is a Power BI project created to analyze employee turnover and understand the key reasons behind employee resignations. This dashboard transforms raw HR data into meaningful visual insights that help HR teams make smarter and data-driven decisions.

The project uses the HR Analytics dataset to study employee behavior based on different factors such as age, salary, department, job role, gender, business travel, and years at company. By analyzing these patterns, organizations can identify high-risk areas of attrition and improve employee retention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition trends in the organization.
* Identify major factors affecting employee turnover.
* Compare attrition across departments, salary groups, and age categories.
* Help HR teams improve employee satisfaction and retention.
* Build an interactive dashboard for quick and easy decision-making.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes multiple visual reports and KPIs that provide a complete overview of employee attrition.

### ✅ Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Employees
* Attrition Count
* Attrition Rate
* Average Age
* Average Salary
* Average Employee Tenure

### 📊 Visual Analysis Included

* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Age Group
* Attrition by Salary Slab
* Attrition by Gender
* Attrition by Job Role
* Attrition by Years at Company
* Employee Distribution Analysis

### 🎛 Interactive Features

* Dynamic Filters and Slicers
* Department-wise Analysis
* Gender and Travel Filters
* User-friendly Dashboard Design
* Easy Navigation and Data Exploration

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Employees aged between 26–35 show the highest attrition rate.
* Employees with lower salaries are more likely to leave the company.
* Sales and Human Resources departments have higher employee turnover.
* Most resignations occur during the early years of employment.
* Frequent business travel may increase attrition chances.
